Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 31, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickNova. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and tools.

2. No Server-Side Storage

The core philosophy of QuickNova is "Client-Side First". This means that for most of our tools (such as Image to PDF, Password Generator, QR Code Generator, etc.), the processing happens entirely within your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

3. Data Collection

We collect minimal data to ensure the website functions correctly:

  • Usage Data: We may use local storage on your device to save your preferences (like Dark Mode or Recent Tools).
  • Analytics: We may use anonymous analytics to understand which tools are most popular, but this data is not linked to your personal identity.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for specific features (e.g., if we integrate a payment provider or external API in the future). Currently, we use:

  • Google Fonts (for typography)
  • Local Browser APIs (for file processing)

5. Cookies

We use cookies and local storage solely for functional purposes, such as remembering your theme preference or favorite tools. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact page.
